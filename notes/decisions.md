# Terminology and Scope Decisions

- Say **marked state** or **marked subspace**, following Grover-algorithm terminology.
- Say **cumulative Born probability**, not “Born quantile.”
- Say that the verifier **marks** a component already present in the uniform superposition; it does not create the component.
- Distinguish `p_*`, the design value, from `p_f`, the actual marked-state weight.
- Use first-person plural in the manuscript.
- State the theorem conditionally and keep assumptions (A1)–(A4) visible.
- Do not call an arbitrary multi-fault history transparent merely because each individual fault is correctable.
- Keep the union-bound factor `L(n)` for the complete logical computation.
- Describe the complexity implication as randomized and separate decision from witness recovery.
