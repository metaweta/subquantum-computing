# Contributing

Contributions are welcome as issues or pull requests, but this repository is a research project whose main results remain under verification.

A substantive contribution should include:

1. a precise statement of the claim being changed;
2. a line-by-line derivation or counterexample;
3. the assumptions used;
4. primary-source references where applicable;
5. a note explaining whether the change affects the theorem, abstract, conclusion, or AI-verification record;
6. tests or arithmetic checks when the change is computational.

Please do not submit purely stylistic edits that obscure the conditional nature of the theorem or the disclosed role of generative AI.
